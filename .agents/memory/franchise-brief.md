## What the Current 3-Page Site Has

### Page 1 — Home (/)
- Hero: "Become a Zivel owner TODAY! 19 Locations and Counting!"
- 3 value tiles: All-in-One, Low Cost, Easy Operations
- "Growing Family" counter: 19 open, 2 under development
- Services grid (8 services)
- Founder quote from Dr. Matt
- Single "Learn More" CTA

### Page 2 — Our Franchise (/our-franchise)
- 4 pillars: Customer Focused, Efficient Operations, Attractive Economics, Straightforward to Build
- Single CTA: "Schedule Call" → Calendly

### Page 3 — Your Dream (/your-dream)
- 5 value props (One-stop Shop, Won't Break the Bank, etc.)
- Financial info block (investment range, minimums, royalty, fee)
- 8-step "Path to Your Franchise" process
- "Coming to Your City" graphic
- Same Calendly CTA

**Key problem:** Financial info and the 8-step sales process — the two strongest trust-builders — are buried on page 3. Zero lead capture; every CTA goes straight to Calendly with no data collected first.

## Recommended Site Outline (zivel.com/franchise)

### 1. Hero Section
- Headline: "Become a Zivel Owner" + subheadline naming "the first non-medical, all-in-one Performance & Recovery franchise"
- Live location counter (from existing location data)
- **Primary CTA:** "Request Franchise Info" → opens Step 1 lead form
- **Secondary CTA:** "Download Franchise Brochure" (PDF gate, email only)
- NO direct Calendly link in hero — scheduling only offered after Step 1 form

### 2. Why Zivel
- Merge Home's 3 tiles + Our Franchise's 4 pillars:
  - All-in-One Model / Customer Focused
  - Low Cost of Entry (with actual numbers)
  - Efficient Operations (1-2 employees at a time)
  - Attractive Economics / multiple income streams
  - Straightforward to Build

### 3. The Numbers / Investment Snapshot
- Move financial block here (near top, not page 3):
  - Initial Investment: $327,400–$429,000
  - Liquidity: $100,000 / Net Worth: $350,000
  - Royalty: 6% | Franchise Fee: $39,500 | Ad Fund: 0%
  - Veterans: $9,500 off franchise fee

### 4. Support & Training Pillars
- Real Estate & Site Selection
- Build-Out & Construction
- Training (initial + ongoing)
- Marketing & Lead Gen (N2ition tech stack differentiator)
- Technology (branded Zivel app + all-in-one scheduling/billing)
- Ongoing Franchisor Support

### 5. Ideal Franchisee Profile *(new content needed)*
- Short bullet list: background, mindset, semi-absentee vs. owner-operator, industry experience not required

### 6. Services Overview (reframed for buyer)
- 8 services, framed around revenue/utilization/repeat visits angle

### 7. Growth & Proof
- Location count + live map
- Growth Rate: +466.7% (Entrepreneur)
- **Ranked #45 — Entrepreneur's 2026 Top New & Emerging Franchises** (feature prominently)

### 7a. Live Territory Map
- Show 19 open locations + any in development + regions open for inquiry
- Built from existing location data on zivel.com (no separate integration)
- Status categories: Open | In Development | Territory Available (no fake urgency tiers)

### 8. Franchisee Success Stories *(new content needed — not blocking launch)*
- 2-4 real franchisees: photo, name, location, quote
- Video testimonial if available

### 9. Path to Your Franchise
- Existing 8-step process moved up prominently:
  Intro Call → Application → Budgeting & Funding → Extended Intro & Education → Territory Selection → Discovery Day & Site Visit → Final Agreement → Onboarding

### 10. FAQ Section *(new content needed)*
- Written in direct Q&A format (optimized for AI answer engines + featured snippets)
- Topics: financing/SBA, semi-absentee logistics, territory exclusivity, multi-unit/master franchise, international

### 11. Financing
- "Zivel doesn't lend directly, but we work with private lenders, SBA, HELOC, and ROBS financing options"

### 12. Master Franchise / Regional Development
- Brief mention within main investment/support section (no standalone page needed yet)

### 13. Contact / Lead Capture — Two-Step Form *(most important change)*

**Step 1 — Initial capture (everyone fills this):**
- Name, email, phone
- City/state/territory of interest
- Investment capability range (dropdown)
- Timeline to open (0-6mo / 6-12mo / 12mo+)
- Prior business or franchise ownership experience?
- Optional: how did you hear about Zivel?

On submit:
- Notification email → matto@zivel.com, subject: "Franchise Lead - Website" (via Resend)
- Lead stored in system (ideally feeds N2ition CRM)

**Step 2 — "Answer a few more questions to schedule your Zoom call with our CEO":**
- Shown immediately after Step 1 submits
- More detail on financing readiness, ideal timeline
- On completion: self-schedule Zoom via Calendly

**Brochure download:** Separate, lowest-commitment option — email only, for visitors not ready for the full form.

### 14. Legal Footer / Disclaimer *(needs franchise attorney)*
- FTC Franchise Rule disclaimer
- List of franchise-registration states
- Confirm exact wording with franchise attorney

## SEO / GEO Structure
- Build as content cluster under zivel.com/franchise with subpages:
  - /franchise/investment
  - /franchise/support
  - /franchise/locations-available
  - /franchise/faq
  - /franchise/master-franchise
- Each subpage answers one specific search query directly (e.g., "How much does a Zivel franchise cost?")
- Consistent entity language: "Zivel," "Zivel franchise," "non-medical performance and recovery franchise"
- FAQ in direct Q&A format for AI answer engines
- Location pages cross-link into franchise funnel ("Interested in bringing Zivel to your city?")
